What we hear from founders

Why your fintech users stop trusting you

None of these are unsolvable. But they're easy to miss when you're deep inside your own product.

🪪
Unclear KYC steps
Users start verification not knowing how long it'll take or what documents they'll need. Each new request feels unexpected — and exhausting.
User feels: confused, anxious
🧾
Hidden fees
They signed up, trusted you, and then discovered a charge they didn't expect. That moment of surprise is often the last interaction they have with your product.
User feels: deceived, let down
🔒
Surprise permissions
A permission request without context reads as a red flag. Even if it's technically necessary, if users don't understand why — they won't say yes.
User feels: surveilled, unsure
⏳
Slow verification
Days of waiting with no updates. Users aren't just bored — they're wondering if something went wrong, and quietly exploring other options while they wait.
User feels: forgotten, uncertain
🫥
Non-transparent flows
Frozen accounts and rejections with no explanation leave users feeling powerless. When people can't understand what happened, they assume the worst.
User feels: powerless, mistrusted
