Why fintech isn’t like your last product

The rules that make consumer apps fun to design are the rules fintech can’t follow.

A growth designer from social or e-commerce walks in expecting to A/B their way to a clean funnel. Then they hit the four things that make this domain its own discipline.

Finding 01

Trust is the product, not a feature

In most apps trust is nice-to-have. Here it is the conversion. When 78% walk after a breach they weren’t even affected by, every pixel either earns confidence or quietly leaks it.

Finding 02

Every flow is regulated

KYC, AML, disclosures, consent — large parts of your UX are dictated by law, not by you. You can’t design them away; you can only design them humane. That’s a different skill.

Finding 03

Friction is mandatory, not optional

“Just remove the steps” is off the table. The work is sequencing required friction so it feels like protection instead of an interrogation — and so users finish anyway.

Finding 04

The cost of bad UX is measured in millions

It’s not a higher bounce rate. It’s lost capital, $73M-a-year compliance overhead, regulator scrutiny and runway you can’t get back. The stakes don’t reset.
